📖 HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Objective: Navigate across all 5 island stages, collect golden coins, avoid traps & deep water, and reach the Stage Portal to answer Informatics questions and collect the 3 Ancient Keys!
⌨️ Keyboard Controls:
• Arrow Left / Right (or A / D): Move Left & Right
• Space (or Arrow Up / W): Jump
📡 BBC micro:bit Controls:
• Button A: Move Left  |  Button B: Move Right
• Shake / A+B: Super Jump & Shield Pulse!
❤️ Lives & Scoring: You have 3 lives. Hitting hazards, falling in the river, or answering a quiz wrongly deducts 1 life. Answer accurately and collect coins for a Legendary rank!
